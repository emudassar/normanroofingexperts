# Competitors & reference sites

## Reference site: leakdetectioncoltonca.com (analysed 2026-10-21)

Another rank-and-rent micro-niche site that Mudassar pointed to as the model.

| What it does | What we copied | What we did not |
|---|---|---|
| First-level URLs (`/slab-leak-detection`, `/leak-detection-reche-canyon`) | Same flat structure | — |
| 9 service pages, 10 area pages, about, contact, blog | 8 services, 5 areas, about, contact | Blog (not needed at launch) |
| No forms anywhere, call buttons only | Calls only | — |
| Email shown in top bar and footer | Email shown | — |
| Street address in footer, "Licensed & insured", "Open 24 hours" | — | Not copied: we have no real address and can't verify licence, insurance or hours |
| Fixed prices ($129/$299/$499) and customer reviews | — | Not copied: invented prices/reviews break our honesty rules and Google's guidelines |
| Homepage ~1,600 words, service pages ~1,400 | — | We go deeper: homepage 3,200+, services 1,500+, areas 1,000+ |
