# Competitor Research — Roofing Contractors, Norman OK

> Research completed. Metrics are Semrush unless noted. Re-check quarterly; SERPs move.

## Headline Finding

**None of the three organic competitors has a single neighbourhood-level location page inside
Norman.** They stop at city level or have no location pages at all. That gap is the core of our
positioning (see `docs/brief.md` §5).

**Second finding:** the Maps 3-pack for the head keyword is saturated with 10+ established,
well-reviewed local contractors — Maupin Roofing, J&M Roofing, Advantage Roofing among them —
several of whom run paid ads on the term. **The map pack is not a realistic target without a
real Google Business Profile.** Organic ranking is the actual goal, and organic is where the
competition is weak.

## Competitor Profiles

### Bohon Roofing

- **URL:** bohonroofingok.com
- **Site platform:** Hibu-built generic template
- **Services listed:** Residential Roofing · Residential Roof Repair · Commercial Roofing ·
  Commercial Roof Repair · Roof Replacement · Hail Damage · Gutter Installation / Repair
- **Service-area pages:** **None at all.**
- **Semrush:** AS 9 · 155 organic traffic/mo · 160 organic keywords · 206 referring domains ·
  354 backlinks
- **On-page strengths:** service coverage is reasonably complete; hail damage is called out
  explicitly, which matches real central-Oklahoma intent.
- **On-page weaknesses / gaps:** generic agency template with little differentiation; no
  geographic content whatsoever; no FAQ depth; thin page copy.
- **Opportunity:** they hold the widest backlink profile of the three (206 RDs) but convert none
  of it into geographic coverage. Every "roofer in [Norman neighbourhood]" query is unclaimed.

## Gap Analysis

**Content gaps we can fill**
- Neighbourhood-level geographic content inside Norman — completely unclaimed by all three.
- Insurance-claim assistance content around storm/hail damage. Central Oklahoma is hail country;
  none of the three treats the claims process as a content asset.
- Real FAQ depth (cost, timeline, insurance, materials) on every service and location page.
- Commercial roofing (Washita has none; ScissorTail's is diluted).

**Location pages competitors are missing**
- Every one. Campus Corner / University North, Downtown Historic District, Brookhaven,
  Hall Park, Adkins Crossing / Summit Lakes, West Norman, Franklin–Denver, plus the surrounding
  towns (Noble, Lexington, Slaughterville, Washington / Little Axe, Goldsby).

**Service pages competitors are missing**
- Storm & hail damage restoration as a standalone, insurance-led page (Washita).
- Commercial roofing (Washita).
- Roof inspection as a genuine lead-capture offer rather than a bullet point.

**Technical / UX weaknesses common across competitors**
- All three are page-builder or template output: heavy, slow, weak semantic structure.
- No structured data depth (Service / FAQPage / BreadcrumbList) observed.
- A fast static Astro build is a straightforward, measurable win on Core Web Vitals.

**Review / reputation gap**
- This is **their** advantage, not ours. All map-pack incumbents have established review
  profiles; we have none and will not fabricate any. We compete on organic coverage and content
  quality, and revisit reputation only if a real contractor tenant is attached.

## Strategic Conclusion

1. **Target organic, not the map pack.** The 3-pack needs a real GBP we don't have.
2. **Win on geographic granularity.** Neighbourhood pages are the wedge no competitor is
   defending.
3. **Win on depth per page.** AS 9 across the board means content quality, not link authority,
   decides these SERPs.
4. **Own the storm/hail + insurance intent cluster.** Highest value, weakest coverage.
