## The residential roofing decisions that actually matter

Most homeowners approach a roof as a single decision: which contractor, at what price. In
practice there are four decisions inside that, and the price differences between bids nearly
always trace to them.

### 1. Tear-off or layover

Laying new shingles over the old ones is cheaper on the day. It also means nobody looked at
the decking that gets sealed up for the next two decades, adds significant dead load,
traps heat that shortens the new shingles' life, and can complicate a future insurance
claim. Ask any bidder whether they tear off as standard practice.

### 2. What happens when the decking is bad

Every honest bid should state a per-sheet rate for replacing sheathing, because nobody knows
how much is bad until the old roof is off. A bid without that number isn't cheaper; it's
just deferring the conversation to a point where you have no leverage.

### 3. Flashing: new or reused

Almost every leak on an otherwise sound roof is a flashing detail. Reusing flashing on a new
roof preserves the old roof's weakest points and is one of the easiest ways to shave a
thousand dollars off a bid invisibly.

### 4. Ventilation

An Oklahoma attic without balanced intake and exhaust can run far above ambient temperature
in summer, cooking the shingles from underneath. This is a leading cause of roofs failing
short of their rated life, and inadequate ventilation voids most manufacturers' material
warranties, meaning the warranty a homeowner was sold on may already be worthless.

## Materials, in plain terms

| Material | Realistic life here | Relative cost | Worth knowing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-tab asphalt shingle | 12-18 years | Lowest | Largely superseded; poorer wind rating |
| Architectural asphalt shingle | 15-22 years | Moderate | The default in Cleveland County; easy and cheap to repair |
| Class 4 impact-resistant shingle | 18-25 years | Moderate-high | Often earns an insurance premium discount; ask your carrier |
| Stone-coated steel | 40+ years | High | Long life; can dent cosmetically under large hail |
| Standing seam metal | 40-60 years | Highest | Excellent longevity; specialist installation; visible denting possible |

Ranges are realistic central-Oklahoma expectations rather than manufacturer ratings, which
assume conditions Oklahoma does not provide.

## What to expect while work happens on your house

Roofing is disruptive. It is loud, there are people above your ceilings all day, and debris
falls. Ask any provider how they handle this:

- Landscaping, AC units and pools covered before tear-off begins
- Debris contained rather than dropped wherever it lands
- Magnetic sweep of yard, drive and street at the end of **every** day, not only the last
- The roof never left open overnight unless it is dried in and weather-tight
- Someone you can actually reach by phone while the job is running
